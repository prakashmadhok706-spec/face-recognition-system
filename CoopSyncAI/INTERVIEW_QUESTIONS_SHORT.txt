--- 1. PROJECT ESSENTIALS ---

Q1: What is CoopSync AI in one line?
A: An end-to-end AI-powered Cooperative ERP combining Dual-Biometric Attendance (Face + QR), Real GPS Geofencing, LMS Learning, Digital Certificates, and AI Job Placement.

Q2: What is the Problem Statement ID and Theme?
A: SIH26087 | Theme: Smart Education / Cooperative Digital Transformation (Ministry of Cooperation / NCCT).

Q3: What is the complete technology stack?
A: Python FastAPI (Backend), React 19 + TypeScript + Vite (Frontend), OpenCV LBPH (Computer Vision), SQLAlchemy + SQLite/Postgres (Database), ReportLab (PDFs), Docker (Deployment).


--- 2. BIOMETRIC ATTENDANCE & ANTI-SPOOFING ---

Q4: Which face recognition algorithm is used and why?
A: OpenCV LBPH (Local Binary Pattern Histogram) with Haar Cascade. Chosen because it runs in <5ms on basic Dual-Core CPUs without any GPU, handles lighting variation, and models train in ~1 second.

Q5: How does the anti-spoofing / liveness check work?
A: Uses Laplacian Variance (second derivative filter). Real live human skin has rich micro-texture yielding variance > 45.0. Flat 2D printed photos and smartphone screens have low texture (<38) and are immediately rejected.

Q6: What is the face confidence formula and rejection threshold?
A: Formula: Confidence = max(35.0, min(99.0, round(100 - (dist * 0.46), 1))).
   Confidence >= 65.0% (Distance <= 76) is accepted; confidence < 65% is rejected as "Unknown Face".


--- 3. GPS GEOFENCING & QR SECURITY ---

Q7: How does GPS Geofencing prevent remote attendance?
A: Backend calculates the Haversine Great-Circle distance between the student's device GPS and the campus coordinates. If the student is > 50 meters away or GPS accuracy is > 100m, attendance is rejected.

Q8: How does the dynamic QR attendance system work?
A: Teacher displays a dynamic QR code that expires in exactly 30 seconds. Each QR has a SHA-256 HMAC cryptographic signature tied to a secret salt and timestamp. Replay scans and screenshot sharing are completely blocked.


--- 4. ERP, CERTIFICATES & AI PLACEMENT ---

Q9: How are digital certificates made fraud-proof?
A: Generated as vector PDFs via ReportLab with an embedded verification QR code linking to `/api/certificates/verify/{cert_no}`. Anyone scanning the QR verifies the authentic record directly from the live database.

Q10: How does the AI Skill-to-Job Matching work?
A: Evaluates set-theoretic intersection between student skills (from ERP & LMS) and corporate job requirements with a baseline market bonus, categorizing candidates as High Match (>=85%), Moderate Match, or Skill Gap.

Q11: Is the AI Advisor chatbot an LLM?
A: It is an intelligent, deterministic semantic parser that reads live database records (attendance %, courses, job openings) with zero cloud cost, 2ms response time, and zero hallucinations.


--- 5. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & FAULT TOLERANCE ---

Q12: What happens if the internet goes down during class?
A: Offline mode automatically buffers attendance in the browser's localStorage. When internet reconnects, it auto-syncs to the backend where GPS and duplicate checks are re-validated.

Q13: How does in-browser face enrollment and retraining work?
A: Captures 50 face samples at 130ms intervals with real-time pose and blur guidance, updates student CSV/DB, and launches a background daemon thread that retrains `classifier.xml` atomically in ~1 second without server restart.

Q14: How would you scale this to 100,000 students across 500 institutes?
A: Replace SQLite with PostgreSQL (PgBouncer), move QR session state to Redis, offload face vector embeddings to a GPU FAISS cluster, and store assets in AWS S3 with Docker/Kubernetes autoscaling.
